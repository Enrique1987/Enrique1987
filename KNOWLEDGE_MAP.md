## Reading status

- **Reviewed:** checked against the page's stated sources and review date.
- **Draft:** exploratory, incomplete, or awaiting verification.
- **Legacy:** retained historical material that may need updating.
- **Project validation:** stated separately, distinguishing local tests from workspace execution.

Learning moves from a captured idea to a connected note, then—when useful—to a reviewed explanation or a reproducible project. A personal reflection does not need to become a portfolio project to be worth keeping.
